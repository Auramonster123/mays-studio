Practice tips

  1. Learn one count at a time. In Learn mode, pause on the first move of the dance and tap Set “1”. Then count ▶ plays exactly one count and freezes on the pose.
  2. Mirror the teacher. If they face you, tap ⇋ Mirror so their left is your left.
  3. Copy side by side. Tap Me in Learn mode to see yourself next to the video.
  4. Drill the hard part. Tap the 8-counts you're working on, hit Loop and slow it to 0.75×. Speed it up once it feels easy.
  5. Slow songs, faster counts. If the BPM feels too slow for how your teacher counts (like 63), double it in the BPM box (126).
  6. Record and compare. Record a take in the mirror, then tap Compare while watching it to see it next to the choreo.
  7. Use your teacher's music. Ask for the exact cut they dance to. No file? Record it with Voice Memos, then Share → Save to Files.
  8. Allow the camera. The first time you open the mirror, tap Allow. If you said no, turn it on in Settings → Safari → Camera.
  9. Saved videos are silent. The music plays when you watch takes here in the app.
  10. Rename a song by tapping its name at the top of the song page.
  11. Keep it on your home screen. That's what keeps your songs, notes and takes saved on your phone.
